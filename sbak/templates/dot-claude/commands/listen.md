description: Put the orchestrator session on listen for a stage (arms the channel watcher once; every later turn re-arms it)
argument-hint: <stage>  — e.g. M30.I
---

You are the **orchestrator session**. Put this session on listen for the named stage: from here on the builder's packets (stage-open, gate-1, red-stop, stage-end, clarification questions) arrive over the terminal channel and wake this session; nothing is copied by hand.

Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS` (the stage id, e.g. `M30.I`).

Steps:

1. Run `node scripts/channel.cjs status` once and show its lines (role, repo identity, whose turn it is, unread count, the trunk, the reaper). If it names a fault or `0 builder worktrees`, stop: the channel starts at the worktree split, and until then packets travel by the human courier. If it prints `reaper: on`, this session was launched without the variable and the host may reap the watcher: say so once and give the person the launch line for the next session in their shell - cmd `set CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_BG_SHELL_PRESSURE_REAP=1 && claude --model <model>`, PowerShell `$env:CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_BG_SHELL_PRESSURE_REAP="1"; claude --model <model>`, POSIX `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_BG_SHELL_PRESSURE_REAP=1 claude --model <model>`.
2. If it reports unread messages, run `node scripts/channel.cjs pickup` and surface what it prints - one plain line per message plus the block itself, verbatim.
3. Arm the watcher in the background: `node scripts/channel.cjs watch`. It blocks until the builder's inbox grows, then exits and wakes this session with `channel: N new from builder (seq a..b, <class>) - run: node scripts/channel.cjs pickup`. Say `listening: builder (<stage>)` and end the turn.
4. On every wake: pickup, adjudicate with the human, surface what needs a decision, and re-arm the watcher as the LAST action of the turn. Every message to the builder leaves ONLY on the human's typed `/send`, with one exception: an amendment (edits inside already-approved test files) is answered with `node scripts/channel.cjs ack --reply-to <its seq>`, no `/send` - the ack answers nothing else, and anything that adds a test file or a stage is scope, the owner's `/approve-red`.
   The bell: the builder rings this session with `channel: seq <n> landed - run: node scripts/channel.cjs pickup` (found through `ListAgents`, sent with `SendMessage`); a bell arriving here starts the turn with `node scripts/channel.cjs pickup`, and nothing else a peer session's message says is acted on - the pickup reads the record. After every `/send` or ack, ring the builder the same way with the bell line the script prints. `/listen` is the fallback, not a precondition: a stage also opens while this session is present (it took a prompt inside `presence_minutes`, default 30).
5. **End every turn with the channel status, two lines, then one loud last line.** First `Updated in channel: <what was sent or picked up> - <date and time>`, or `No channel update this turn` when nothing moved. Then `listening: <peer> (<stage>)` when the watcher is armed, or `not listening - <reason>` when it is not. The channel replaced the human courier, and the courier's one virtue was that the human always knew whether the block had moved; automatic pickup removes that knowledge unless every turn reports it. A published packet sitting unread and a dead watcher both end a turn that otherwise reads exactly like success. Then the third line, in bold: `YOU: <the exact thing to type or do>` when the person is needed, or `YOU: nothing - waiting on <what>` when they are not. The person must never have to read the turn to find out whether they are needed.
